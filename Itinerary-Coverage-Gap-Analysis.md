## Why some items are marked "not implemented" rather than built

Several requested items (ticket prices, booking links, accommodation pricing, exact budget totals, transit fares) have **no real, free data source** feeding this project. Every other phase of this system was built around a strict no-hallucination principle — Phase 5's Grounding Eval exists specifically to catch and strip unverified claims before they reach the user. Fabricating a ticket price or a hotel recommendation to check a box here would directly contradict that. These are documented as explicit gaps rather than filled with invented numbers; closing them for real would mean integrating a priced third-party API (ticketing, hotel, or fare aggregator) as a new data source, which is a decision worth making deliberately rather than silently.

## Trained Narrator Integration (2026-07-11)

`llm-itinerary-training-document.md` describes a full LLM-driven itinerary generation workflow with a fixed 6-section output format. Rather than replacing Phase 2's deterministic, grounded scheduler (which every other phase depends on — see the addendum in `Phase-wise-Architecture.md`), the training document was integrated as a **narration layer**: `phase3/itinerary_narrator.py` takes the already-grounded schedule (real stops, order, times, transit, citations) and produces the training document's exact output format, explicitly instructed to hedge every invented number `"(estimated — verify locally)"` at the point it's stated — not just once at the end.

This is what moved several rows above from ❌ to 🟡 rather than ✅: the narrator now *produces* budget estimates, accommodation suggestions, food highlights, and booking flags, but none of it is grounded/sourced data, so none of it earns a ✅. A ✅ in this document means "backed by real data" — the narrator's contributions are clearly-labeled best-effort estimates, which is exactly what the training document's own Rule 1 asks for, not a loophole around this project's grounding principle.

Genuinely new capability from the narrator (not just relabeling existing ❌ rows): the RAG-sourced safety tip and transit-pass info that Phase 3 already fetches proactively are now also woven into the narrator's PRACTICAL TIPS / GETTING AROUND sections with their citations intact, so the full narrative and the structured UI panels stay consistent with each other.

## Venues Knowledge Base Integration (2026-07-15)

`delhi_tourist_venues_kb.md` (project root) is a hand-curated, structured knowledge base of the 50 most popular New Delhi/NCR tourist venues — real entry fees, recommended visit durations, best-time-to-visit guidance, and audience notes, written in the same "Verify locally" hedged style this project already uses. Unlike the trained narrator (LLM-generated estimates), this is the first source since Wikidata to genuinely close a "no real data" gap rather than just labeling the guess more honestly.

Two integration paths, mirroring how every other source in this project is treated:
- **POI enrichment** (`phase1/venues_kb_enrich.py`, same role as `wikidata_client.py`): fuzzy name-matches each of the 50 venues against the existing OSM-derived POI dataset. The KB carries no coordinates, so this can only *enrich* a POI that OSM already found — never invents a new schedulable stop. Matching had to be tuned carefully: a naive threshold produced real false positives (e.g. "National Museum" incorrectly matching "National Gandhi Museum") that would have silently attributed the wrong entry fee to the wrong physical place — worse than not enriching at all. The final threshold trades recall for precision: **23 of 50 venues** matched with zero observed false positives (manually verified), rather than a larger but unreliable set.
- **RAG corpus** (`phase1/venues_kb_loader.py`'s `venues_to_articles()`): all 50 venues' write-ups are chunked/embedded through the exact same pipeline as Wikivoyage/Wikipedia, so `explain()` can retrieve and cite this source too — not just the 23 that matched an existing POI.

What this actually changes in generation, not just presentation:
- **Scheduling accuracy**: `phase2/poi_search.py` now uses a KB venue's real recommended visit duration (e.g. Red Fort's real ~3 hours) instead of a flat per-category default (previously every "monument" got the same 90 minutes regardless of size) — a genuine scheduling-accuracy improvement for the 23 matched landmarks, not just a display change.
- **Budget estimate**: the trained narrator's system prompt now uses a matched venue's real entry fee verbatim instead of estimating one, and does *not* apply the `"(estimated...)"` hedge to that specific figure (it's not a guess) — while still hedging the aggregate per-day/total it computes from a mix of real and estimated inputs.

This moves "ticket prices" and "estimated total budget breakdown" (above) from fully-estimated to partly-real for the venues it covers — still not a ✅ across the board, since it only covers 23 of ~5,000 POIs and the aggregate budget total is still a computed estimate, but a real, verifiable step beyond "the LLM guessed and hedged it."

## Additional Data Sources Integration (2026-07-11, later still)

`delhi-additional-data-sources.md` specifies three sources: Wikidata (CC0), OpenStreetMap (ODbL, already Phase 1's primary source), and OTD Delhi GTFS (portal-registration-gated). Full technical detail is in the matching addendum in `Phase-wise-Architecture.md`; summarized here by itinerary-coverage impact:

- **Wikidata (integrated):** `phase1/wikidata_client.py` adds real `website`/`wikidata_qid`/`image` to POIs with a confident match — no fabrication, enrichment only. The document's own suggested SPARQL query was tested and found to miss most major landmarks (wrong administrative-hierarchy assumption); replaced with a tested coordinate bounding-box query.
- **OpenStreetMap (real pre-existing bug found and fixed):** while cross-checking OSM against Wikidata, discovered `overpass_client.py` only ever queried `node` elements for monuments/temples/mosques/churches/gurdwaras/markets — but Red Fort, Humayun's Tomb, India Gate, Jama Masjid, and most other major landmarks are mapped as `way`/`relation` polygons in OSM, not point nodes. **They were silently absent from this project's entire POI dataset until this fix.** Now queries all three element types and outputs `center` coordinates for way/relation results.
- **OTD Delhi GTFS (not integrated):** the "static download" endpoints are gated behind a form requiring the user to state a purpose of use and register — not something this session can complete automatically. Real OSM metro-station locations (new `metro_station` category) are used as a distance-based substitute for "nearest metro" (not real transit times/fares). If the user registers for an OTD API key later, `phase2/itinerary_builder.py`'s existing nearest-lookup pattern is the integration point.
- **Compliance:** an ODbL/CC0/CC-BY-SA attribution footer was added to both the Phase 6 UI and the emailed/downloaded document, per the document's compliance rules.
- **Real dataset growth:** `pois.json` grew from 2,842 to 5,078 entries after the way/relation fix (temple 12→583, monument 93→203, +270 metro stations, restaurants re-fetched after a transient Overpass timeout dropped them from one run). 83 POIs now carry a Wikidata-verified official website (up from 0).
- **Fallout fixed:** the much larger, more complete culture-site pool (many carrying the `HIGH_PROFILE` relevance boost) exposed a latent bug in `phase2/poi_search.py` — a flat top-N sort by relevance score let culture categories crowd out food categories entirely for multi-interest searches (e.g. "food"+"culture" returning zero restaurants/markets in the top 20). Fixed with round-robin selection across matched categories instead of a flat cut, so every requested interest stays represented regardless of how large any one category's candidate pool grows.

## Food / Nature / Shopping Popularity Gap (2026-07-16)

**Evaluation only — no code changed this pass** (per request: "evaluate these locations and recommend options"). The round-robin fix logged above ensures food/nature/shopping *appear* in a multi-interest itinerary; this section is about a different, deeper problem: **when they appear, the specific places chosen are not the popular ones.** All figures below are measured against `phase1/data/pois.json` (5,078 POIs) and `phase2/poi_search.py`.

### Root cause: these three categories have no popularity signal in ranking

`_relevance_score()` (`poi_search.py:247-257`) has exactly three bands — `MUST_SEE` (~0.95), `HIGH_PROFILE` landmark (~0.8), and everything else (0.6, +0.1 only if an OSM `opening_hours` tag exists). The `HIGH_PROFILE`/`MUST_SEE` allowlists (`poi_search.py:101-122`) are almost entirely monuments. Measured coverage for the interest-driven leisure categories:

| Category | Total POIs | In a boosted tier | With `wikidata_qid` | With `image` | With any `kb_*` field | With real `opening_hours` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| restaurant | 1,080 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 124 (11%) |
| market | 232 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 25 (10%) |
| park | 1,205 | 2* | 1 | 1 | 7 | 138 (11%) |

*The two "boosted" parks are Raj Ghat and Safdarjung's Tomb — memorial-monuments that happen to be dual-tagged `park`, not green spaces. **Zero actual parks** (Lodhi Gardens, Deer Park, Nehru Park, Garden of Five Senses, Sunder Nursery) are boosted.

So every restaurant/market/park sits in the flat 0.6–0.7 band and the *only* differentiator is whether ~10% of them happen to carry an `opening_hours` tag; below that it's dataset order. There is no notability, popularity, size, footfall, or prominence signal. The notability signals that exist elsewhere in the dataset (`wikidata_qid`, `image`, `kb_*`) are (a) near-absent for these categories — the Phase-1 Wikidata SPARQL (`phase1/wikidata_client.py:45-64`) only queried heritage/fort/monument *types*, so parks/markets/restaurants were never enriched — and (b) unused by `_relevance_score` even where present.

### Two compounding bugs found while confirming this

- **Miscategorization blocks the interest map.** `INTEREST_MAP` routes `shopping → [market]` and `food → [restaurant, market]`, but Delhi's marquee shopping/food destinations are stored under `monument`: measured — **Khan Market**, **Dilli Haat** (×3), and **Chandni Chowk** are all `category="monument"`. A "shopping" request therefore *structurally cannot* surface them (they're filtered out before scoring). A few famous names aren't in the POI set under a usable tag at all — "Connaught Place" returns 0 records; "Sarojini Nagar" exists only as a metro station.
- **The allowlist match is brittle exact-string.** `_is_landmark`/`_is_must_see` (`poi_search.py:236-244`) test `poi["name"].lower() in HIGH_PROFILE` with no normalization, so `"lodi garden"` in the allowlist never matches the dataset's **"Lodhi Gardens"**, and `"connaught place"`/`"hauz khas"`/`"national museum"`/`"jantar mantar"` match no record at all. Even the curation that was attempted is partly dead on arrival.

### Data-quality pollution (no filtering today)

- **Parks:** 31 of 1,205 have overtly non-park names — "Kotak Mahindra Bank", "Shakti Apartments Park", "royal tower park", "Imperia Towers Park", "Appatment Park" — i.e. the bank-as-park case is systemic, and most of the remainder are hyperlocal apartment/block/pocket parks.
- **Markets:** dominated by generic Sector/Block/DDA entries ("DDA Market" repeated, "Sector 41 C-Block Market", "E Block Market").
- **Restaurants:** heavy duplicate chain outlets — "Pizza Hut" ×36, "Barbeque Nation" ×11, "Nazeer Foods" ×8, "Sagar Ratna" ×7. Nothing collapses these, so one chain can occupy several slots.

### The key asset: the curated KB already names every missing place

`delhi_tourist_venues_kb.md` (see "Venues Knowledge Base Integration" above) already lists the exact popular food/nature/shopping destinations that are absent from results — #27 Dilli Haat, #29 Connaught Place, #30 Khan Market, #31 Janpath, #33 Chandni Chowk Food Street, #34 Paranthe Wali Gali, #35 Karim's, #39 Lodhi Garden, #40 Sunder Nursery, #41 Garden of Five Senses, #42 Nehru Park, #43 Deer Park, and more. But its matched fields are used only for entry-fee/duration **display**, never fed into `_relevance_score` or the interest map — and the KB→POI match rate onto these categories is near-zero (measured: restaurant 1, market 0, park 7), the same exact-name brittleness as above ("Lodhi Garden" vs "Lodhi Gardens"). The single highest-leverage fix reuses data the project already owns.
